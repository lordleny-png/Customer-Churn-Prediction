# Customer-Churn-Prediction
## Project Title: CUSTOMER CHURN PREDICTION
                  This project analyzes customer churn in the telecom sector using data-driven techniques to predict which customers are likely to leave. As a business analytics fresher, I built an end-to-end solution involving data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualization, and machine learning modeling. The goal is to create actionable insights that telecom companies can use to improve customer retention, ultimately boosting revenue and reducing operational costs. Key deliverables include a predictive model, interactive visualizations, and a business report with recommendations.

## Business problem: 
          Customer churn is a major pain point for telecom companies, leading to significant revenue loss—industry estimates suggest churn costs up to 5-10% of annual revenue. In this project, we address the challenge of predicting churn to enable proactive retention. Specifically:
- **Problem Statement**: Identify at-risk customers based on historical data to prevent them from switching providers.
- **Business Impact**: By predicting churn with high accuracy, companies can target interventions (e.g., personalized offers or loyalty programs), potentially increasing customer lifetime value (CLV) by 15-20% and reducing churn rates by 10-15%.
- **Stakeholders**: Marketing managers, product teams, and executives who rely on data to drive decisions. For example, if the model flags a high-value customer, the business can offer a discount to retain them, directly impacting profitability.

This aligns with real BA roles, where we translate data into strategies that save money and enhance customer experience.
